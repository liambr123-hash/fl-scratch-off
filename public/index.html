What does “estimated payout” mean?

Expected payout per dollar across a modeled remaining ticket pool. Unsold ticket counts are not public. We estimate inventory from the fraction of prizes unclaimed; this assumption can be wrong. A ticket returning less than $1 per $1 has a modeled loss.

Are these the current odds of winning?

Overall and profit odds come from the published prize structure. Estimated remaining-ticket payouts use unclaimed prize data, which can include already sold tickets awaiting a claim. They are different measurements and should not be treated as guaranteed current odds.

Why do two tickets with the same name differ?

Different game numbers identify different releases. Always match the game number on your ticket. Each release has its own prize counts, published odds and deadlines.

Does a winning retailer improve my chances?

Historical winner locations reflect sales volume and reporting, not a store being due for a win. We do not know a retailer’s current ticket inventory and do not recommend stores based on past winners.

What happens when the data looks wrong?

We reconcile advertised prize tiers with counts, label source limitations and exclude invalid odds records from recommendation lists. Source updates can lag. Always check the official game page before buying.

How is this site funded?

Optional Ko-fi support and clearly labeled sponsorships. Sponsors cannot buy a higher ticket ranking or change the calculations. Core comparisons are free. Cloudflare provides aggregate traffic and performance analytics.
