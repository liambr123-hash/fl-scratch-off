About & methodology

An independent project that makes Florida's public scratch-off data easier to read — published odds, remaining prizes, estimated payout, and reported claims. Core comparisons are free, with optional support and clearly labeled sponsorships.

Where the numbers come from

  • Games, odds & prizes remaining — the Florida Lottery's own public game data feed, refreshed nightly. Every prize tier's odds, total, remaining, and paid counts are theirs, unmodified.
  • Winners — the Florida Lottery's public "Top-Prize Winners with Retailer Information" reports, parsed automatically. Recent winners of $250,000+ may show as anonymous for 90 days under Florida Statute 24.1051, then their names appear.
  • Retailers — the Florida Lottery's public store locator, sampled statewide (13,000+ locations).
  • Home/store locations — mapped to city/ZIP centroids (GeoNames); county populations are U.S. Census estimates.

How the analysis works

  • Expected value (EV/$) is the average payout per $1 for a random remaining ticket. Every game is negative-EV as designed; the best typically sit around $0.80–0.92, and a nearly sold-out game can briefly turn positive on its remaining tickets — we flag those. Figures use advertised face values — most jackpot winners take the cash option at roughly 60–65% of face, so real returns run lower.
  • Estimated tickets are inferred from each tier's odds × prize count (robust to missing tiers). Games with decommissioned/degenerate odds tables are shown without EV.
  • Retailer "lift" is winners ÷ that chain's actual store count, versus the statewide average. It's a sales-volume proxy, not luck — every ticket is independent and random. No store or town is "due."

The Value Score (open formula)

Each on-sale game gets a 0–100 score — unlike other sites, the math is public:

  • 45% — current expected value per $1 (scaled 0.55→0.95)
  • 20% — "profit odds": the chance one ticket pays back more than it cost (design odds, tiers above ticket price; scaled 3%→15%)
  • 20% — freshness: % of total prize value still unclaimed
  • 15% — jackpot health: a 0.5 baseline plus 0.5 × the share of advertised top prizes still out there; zero when none remain (those games are capped at "Avoid")

Bands: 70+ Excellent · 55–69 Good · 40–54 Fair · under 40 Avoid. The score ranks relative value only — every scratch-off remains negative expected value.

The honest disclaimers

Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the Florida Lottery or the State of Florida. This is a fan-made statistics project. All game names and figures are referenced factually from public records.

Odds and prize counts change constantly — always verify on the official Florida Lottery site or app before buying. Nothing here improves your odds; scratch-offs are a form of entertainment with a built-in house edge, and every ticket's outcome is independent.

Play responsibly. If gambling is causing harm, call the Florida Council on Compulsive Gambling: 1-888-ADMIT-IT (1-888-236-4848). Must be 18+ to play.

Open data and clear update times

The comparison pages are generated as readable HTML from a shared data snapshot. An automated daily refresh rebuilds the homepage, individual game pages, downloads, and search sitemap. The displayed snapshot date records the source build; a design update does not make old data look new. If a source fails validation, the site keeps the last usable snapshot.

The code and data pipeline are public. You can download the full snapshot or game data as CSV. Published prize counts, model estimates, and source limitations are labeled separately.

Traffic analytics and privacy

Cloudflare provides aggregate traffic and performance analytics and operates the site's network infrastructure. The site has no user accounts or advertising trackers. Its comparison interface does not ask for your contact details.

Opening Ko-fi takes you to Ko-fi's own service and privacy terms. The optional sponsorship form sends only the information described below to this site's private inquiry database.

Support and sponsorships

Optional Ko-fi support helps cover the work. Sponsorships will be clearly labeled. Sponsors cannot pay for a higher ticket ranking, alter the calculations, or control the conclusions. Core comparisons remain free.

To discuss a sponsorship, use the sponsorship inquiry form. It stores your name, company or project, reply email, message, optional budget, consent version, submission time, and a reference ID in a private Cloudflare D1 database. This information is available to the project owner for reviewing and replying to the inquiry; it is not published or added to a mailing list. The form does not store your IP address, browser user agent, or referrer in that database.

Inquiries older than 30 days are removed by daily cleanup and cleanup on new submissions. Submitting the form saves the inquiry for private review; it does not automatically send email or guarantee an immediate reply. Any reply is handled separately by the project owner using the contact information you provide.
